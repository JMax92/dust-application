Orbit

My executive assistant for work and life.

Replies ready before I open the message.

I run our US business and Bogotá office, across several teams and time zones. Orbit prepares email, Slack and WhatsApp replies before I see the messages. I decide what gets sent.

It gathers the tasks and news I need for the day. Less time catching up, more time for clients, the team and the decisions that need me.

What it keeps in one place

  • Messages, calendar, tasks and team decisions.
  • Client news and activity that can help the sales team.
  • Changes in Colombian laws relevant to running the office.
  • Personal plans, learning and projects outside work.
  • Screen captures of my own work, so it can learn how I work.

Sales Copilot

Personal outreach, follow-up and coaching.

A tool the team uses every day.

Writing a useful first message takes time. Generic AI drafts often needed too much editing, and the team still had to work out how to follow up.

I built Sales Copilot for our sellers, managers and account managers. It uses the prospect’s context to draft LinkedIn outreach and the next message, whether they reply or not. It explains the suggestion, so the seller learns while reviewing it.

Maya · Research team lead

Public context: finding a suitable specialist quickly is difficult.

First approach

Hi Maya, saw your note about finding specialists quickly. Which is usually harder: finding the right person or getting them available in time?

Why this message

Start with a problem she mentioned. Ask one simple question before explaining the service.

If she replies

That makes sense. Is the time usually lost finding someone suitable, or coordinating the call once you’ve found them?

If she doesn’t reply

Hi Maya, one follow-up on this. Do you usually need people for very specific questions, or a broader view of the market?

Fictional prospect and examples. A walkthrough, not a screenshot or captured output from the private tool.
