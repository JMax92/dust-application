Sales Copilot

A better first message.
A consistent conversation after it.

Built for the people doing the selling.

A generic AI message is easy to spot. Our sellers needed something that used a prospect’s context and helped them follow through, not just write an opening line.

I built Sales Copilot for sales associates, managers and account managers. It prepares personalised approaches, suggests follow-ups after a reply or silence, and explains the reasoning so the seller learns as they go.
